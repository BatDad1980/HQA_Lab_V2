# Non-Claim Boundary Memo
**Classification:** STRICT COMMERCIAL SEPARATION

## Objective
This memo establishes the exact technological boundaries of the Homeostatic Quantum Architecture (HQA V2) proxy stack.

## What HQA V2 IS:
1. **A Modular Proxy Stack**: HQA sits between high-level logical instructions and physical execution hardware.
2. **A Topology-Aware Router**: HQA utilizes A* logic to map physical connections on a sparse-lattice grid, mathematically dodging "hot" or degraded qubits.
3. **A Safety Governor**: HQA employs a strict policy gate that prevents unauthorized or thermally hazardous commands from reaching the physical cryostat or HAL.
4. **An Asymmetric Error Handler**: (Alice & Bob specific) HQA is capable of routing based exclusively on phase-flip ($Z$ error) thermal gradients when operating on hardware that autonomously suppresses bit-flips (cat qubits).

## What HQA V2 IS NOT (The Non-Claims):
1. **NOT Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)**: HQA possesses no self-awareness, consciousness, or broad generalizing capabilities. It is a deterministic, reflex-driven control plane.
2. **NOT a "Hacked" Quantum Supremacy Solver**: HQA does not violate the laws of physics or produce impossible coherence times. It manages existing coherence constraints more efficiently.
3. **NOT "Unhackable"**: No system is unhackable. HQA employs zero-trust isolation and localized quenching to minimize attack surfaces, but we make no liability-free guarantees.

Reviewers should evaluate HQA purely on its merits as an edge-optimized, hardware-safe control plane.
